1. Information We Collect

Citi Group collects personal and financial information to provide banking services, satisfy legal Know-Your-Customer (KYC) obligations, and protect your accounts against fraud.

  • Identity Data: Full legal name, date of birth, government identification numbers (SSN/Tax ID, passport details).
  • Contact Data: Residential address, registered email address, telephone numbers.
  • Financial Data: Account balances, transaction history, wire transfer beneficiary details, credit scores.
  • Technical & Biometric Data: IP addresses, browser fingerprinting, device identifiers, and biometric authorization tokens.

2. How We Use Information

We process your information under recognized lawful bases, including the fulfillment of our banking agreement, compliance with statutory obligations, and legitimate institutional interests:

  • Executing domestic and cross-border payment orders and settlements.
  • Conducting automated sanctions screening, Anti-Money Laundering (AML) monitoring, and fraud prevention.
  • Delivering customer support, statements, notifications, and security alerts.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We deploy end-to-end 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. All database vaults reside in certified Tier-4 secure banking enclaves subject to continuous intrusion testing, physical access controls, and FINMA/FDIC data handling standards.

4. Information Sharing & Transfers

We do not sell personal data to third parties. We may disclose information only to correspondent clearing banks, card payment networks (Visa/Mastercard), regulatory supervisors, or law enforcement entities when required by lawful subpoena or international treaty.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. EU GDPR, Swiss FADP, California CCPA), you hold statutory rights to access, rectify, restrict, or request portability of your stored personal records. Contact our Data Protection Officer to submit a verified data subject request.
