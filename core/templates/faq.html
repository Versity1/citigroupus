Getting Started & Account Opening

How do I open an account with Citi Group? expand_more
Opening an account is entirely digital. Click "Open Account" at the top of the page, provide your legal name and contact details, and verify your email. The entire onboarding process takes under 3 minutes with instant account number generation.
What documents are required to open an account? expand_more
You will need a government-issued photo ID (passport, national ID, or driver's license) and a valid proof of residential address (utility bill or bank statement issued within the last 90 days).

Transfers & International Wires

How long do international wire transfers take to settle? expand_more
Most international wires settle same-day or within 1 business day via the global SWIFT network. Internal transfers between Citi Group accounts settle instantaneously 24/7.
What is the Tax Verification Number (TVN) compliance check? expand_more
High-value cross-border transactions may undergo automated compliance clearance. In such instances, a valid Tax Verification Number (TVN) ensures full international AML and tax transparency. You can request or validate TVNs directly in your compliance dashboard.

Security & Account Protection

How does Citi Group protect my financial assets? expand_more
Deposits are protected under applicable regulatory frameworks including FDIC insurance up to $250,000 for US accounts and FINMA capital safeguards for European accounts. Every session is encrypted with 256-bit SSL and safeguarded by automated fraud monitors.
How do I enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)? expand_more
Navigate to your Profile settings after logging in. Under the Security section, toggle Two-Factor Authentication to active. You will receive one-time PIN codes via registered email/SMS for all sensitive transaction authorizations.
